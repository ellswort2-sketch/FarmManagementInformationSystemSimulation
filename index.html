Guided practice

Build a useful farm record

Use simulated information for practice, or observations authorized by your instructor. Never present invented measurements as actual field data.

  1. Identify yourself. In Submit & back up, enter your name or instructor-approved student ID and class/section.
  2. Review the two fields. Open Fields & crops. Read each field’s crop, area, and source. You may add a field for an instructor-approved site.
  3. Add three soil observations. Select Soil observation and choose a field. Enter one record for each of volumetric soil moisture (%), bulk electrical conductivity (dS/m), and soil temperature (°C). Identify the sample location, depth, method/instrument, and source. If using practice values, choose Simulated.
  4. Add an activity record. Record or plan an irrigation, scouting, or other operation. Identify what occurred or is proposed, the location, and relevant quantity and units. Choose Planned when it has not happened; completed simulated activities remain Simulated.
  5. Check your data. Review dates, units, coordinates, depth, source, and unusual values. Edit errors while documenting the correction in the notes. Do not replace an unknown value with a guess.
  6. Interpret your evidence. Write a brief management question, describe a pattern or limitation, and identify one additional measurement you would need. Different dates, depths, and instruments may limit comparisons.
  7. Submit your work. Download the student report and records CSV. Keep a JSON backup. Upload the requested files to Canvas as directed by your instructor.

Coordinates are optional for this prototype. If you use them, enter WGS 84 decimal degrees (EPSG:4326), latitude first. Do not enter UTM easting/northing here. The schematic does not plot coordinates.
